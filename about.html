How We Started

A Home Away From Home for Every Budget

What began as a welcoming 15-room family lodge near the central transit station has grown into the city's most trusted Budget Hotel & Lodge.

Whether you are visiting for an overnight business meeting, a family holiday, medical transit, or a student group tour, our housekeeping staff and front desk team take pride in making every room feel fresh, safe, and inviting.

Spotless Cleanliness

Hospital-grade room sanitization, fresh cotton bed linens, and sparkling private bathrooms.

Honest Nightly Tariffs

Our Journey

Haven Rooms Milestones

Growing from a neighborhood transit lodge into a full-facility budget hotel trusted by thousands of travelers.

2014

Founding of HavenStay Lodge

Opened our doors with 15 clean economy rooms near the central railway station, offering travelers safe, affordable overnight stays.

2017

Deluxe AC Wing & Family Suites

Expanded to 35 rooms by adding a new air-conditioned wing, interconnected family quad suites, and modern elevator access.

2021

Homestyle Cafe & Fiber Wi-Fi

Launched our ground-floor homestyle dining cafe, high-speed mesh Wi-Fi across all floors, and gated EV-ready parking.

2026

Top-Rated City Budget Hotel

Celebrated hosting over 50,000 satisfied guests with a 4.9-star cleanliness and value rating across 48 modern rooms.
